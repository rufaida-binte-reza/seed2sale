About Seed2Sale

Seed2Sale bridges the gap between farmers and consumers by creating a transparent, sustainable agricultural marketplace. We empower local farmers with fair trade tools, logistics support, and digital visibility — ensuring fresh produce reaches households directly.

  • Fair pricing and direct contracts with farmers.
  • Transparent traceability and eco-friendly supply chains.
  • Digital tools for farm-to-market operations.

Our Mission

To empower farmers through technology, fair trade, and community-driven growth — connecting every harvest directly to the consumer.

Our Vision

A transparent, equitable agricultural ecosystem where every farmer thrives and every buyer trusts the source of their food.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Seed2Sale support local farmers? ▼

We provide digital tools, transparent contracts, and fair pricing directly connecting farmers to buyers without middlemen.

Can I track where my produce comes from? ▼

Yes! Our traceability feature lets you view origin, farmer details, and quality information on every product page.

What payment methods are accepted? ▼

We accept bKash, Nagad, debit/credit cards, and cash on delivery (COD).
